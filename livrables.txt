Deliverable 2: Intelligence Gathering ⏰ Due: Tuesday May 12th at 23:59



The clock is ticking. This isn't a research paper; it’s a mission briefing. We need to see your "stack." The goal of this checkup is to ensure you aren't building your analysis on a foundation of sand. Show us your raw materials and the tools you are using to mine them.



A. The OSINT Framework
How are you gathering your intel? We want to see the methodology.

Source Mapping: List the specific open-source intelligence (OSINT) channels you are leveraging (e.g., social listening, public financial filings, satellite imagery, web scraping, or industry forums).
Tooling: Which tools are in your belt? (e.g., Python libraries, Google Dorks, specialized search engines). Explain why these are the right tools for this specific target.


B. Dataset Inventory
Show us the "Gold" you’ve found.

The Catalog: List the primary datasets you have successfully secured.
Data Provenance: Where did this data come from, and how fresh is it?
The "So What": For each major dataset, write one sentence on the specific signal it provides for your chosen subject.


C. Intelligence Gaps & Risks
Be honest about what you don't know yet.

The Missing Link: What data point is currently missing that would make your project a "home run"?
Mitigation: How do you plan to bypass this gap? (e.g., using a proxy metric or a different OSINT technique).


Format: PDF, 1 page max. Use a table or bulleted list for the datasets.
