## Sprint Format — What to Expect

### 1. What is a Sprint BDD?
* **An intense hackathon** compressed into 2+ days, with multiple subjects running in parallel across teams.
* **Work under pressure** on a topic far outside your comfort zone — just as real operational environments demand.
* **Groups are assigned** by the admin team — you do not choose your colleagues. Each group tackles one case among the 5 proposed by Marine Nationale.
* **1st Sprint BDD Synchronized:** Synchronized with the Marseille campus; students from both campuses compete on the same subjects.

### 2. What are you expected to deliver?
* At the end of the Sprint, each team must **defend their MVP or key findings** in front of Marine Nationale experts and a jury — on **May 13th afternoon**.
* The goal is not perfection — it is **speed, rigour, and the ability to communicate under constraints**.
